# Target List and Contact Plan

*Twenty-eight counterparties in three tiers, with the specific capacity Tesla is after, the one-sentence hook for the first email, what to offer, who to call first, and what to watch. Names and titles are from company filings, press releases, earnings transcripts, and investor materials dated 2025–2026; the source for each person is in `contacts.csv`. No email addresses are listed unless they were printed on a company page we read, and none are guessed. Capacity figures are gross unless marked IT. Current to November 8, 2026.*

**How to read the tiers.** Tier 1 is energized or near-energized, uncontracted capacity in ERCOT and SPP where a Megapod deal can produce rent in 2027. Tier 2 is large 2027–28 blocks where the Megapack kicker opens the door, plus power-first developers under financial pressure. Tier 3 is neoclouds and non-US or small-ticket hosts where the hosted-services variant fits.

## Tier 1 — Wave 1 (first 45 days)

- **Hook:** "Your Corsicana lease already prices powered land with tenant-owned compute modules; we would like the same structure on the next Rockdale block, starting in 2027, with Tesla funding everything past your switchgear."
- **Offer:** Variant A on 100–200 MW of Rockdale mining capacity; Megapack allocation for Riot's remaining Rockdale and Kentucky load.
- **Contacts:** Jonathan Gibbs, Chief Data Center Officer (primary; ex-Prime Data Centers). Jason Chung, CFO (economics; retains corp dev and IR). Jason Les, CEO. Benjamin Yi, Executive Chairman (sponsor-level).
- **Watch:** Riot's AMD-related loan did not close on schedule; its forward power contract at Corsicana has been flagged as a liability. Expect a counter anchored on $2M+ per IT MW.
