# 07 — Interview Prep

Prepare the candidate for interviews for a specific role.

## Inputs

- The posting + company research (WebSearch: products, stack, interview process
  if findable on Glassdoor/Levels/blogs).
- Candidate profile (`01`) and behavioral profile (`02`, especially STAR
  stories).

## Produce

1. **Likely questions**, grouped:
   - **Behavioral** — map each to a STAR story from `02`. If a common question
     has no matching story, flag the gap and help draft one.
   - **Role-specific / technical** — derived from the posting's requirements.
   - **Company-specific** — based on their product, mission, recent news.
2. **STAR answers** — concise Situation/Task/Action/Result for the top
   behavioral questions, in the candidate's voice (`03`).
3. **Talking points** — 3–5 things to proactively work in (signature
   achievements, relevant projects).
4. **Questions to ask them** — thoughtful, specific to the company/role (signals
   research and seriousness).
5. **Logistics checklist** — format (phone/video/onsite), who's interviewing,
   time, what to prepare.

## Style

Keep answers tight and honest — practice material, not a script to memorize.
Encourage the candidate to make each story their own.
