# 03 — Writing Style

Rules for all generated resumes, cover letters, and outreach. US English.

## Voice

- Direct, confident, specific. First person for cover letters.
- Lead with impact and outcomes, not responsibilities.
- Quantify wherever honestly possible (%, $, time, scale).

## Banned / avoid

- **No em-dashes (—).** Use commas, periods, or parentheses.
- No clichés: "results-driven", "team player", "think outside the box",
  "passionate about", "synergy", "hit the ground running", "wear many hats".
- No filler hedging: "I believe", "I feel like", "sort of".
- No invented metrics or credentials. If unknown, omit — never fabricate.

## Resume bullets

- Pattern: **[Strong action verb] + [what] + [quantified result]**.
- Past tense for past roles, present tense for current.
- One line each where possible; ATS parses plain text best.

## Cover letters

- 3–4 short paragraphs, under one page.
- Open with a specific hook about the company/role (proves research).
- Middle: connect 2–3 concrete experiences to stated requirements.
- Close with a clear, low-pressure call to action and a signature line.

## Tone calibration

Match the company's register: more formal for finance/healthcare/gov, more
relaxed for startups — but always professional and concrete.
