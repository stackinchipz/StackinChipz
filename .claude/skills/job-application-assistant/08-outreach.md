# 08 — Outreach & Networking

Templates and rules for outreach messages. Always apply `03-writing-style.md`
(no em-dashes, no clichés, concrete and specific) and ground every claim in
`01`/`02`. Never invent shared connections or mutual interests.

## Channel constraints

| Channel | Limit | Tone |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn connection note | **300 chars** | Warm, specific, low-ask |
| LinkedIn InMail / DM | ~1–2 short paragraphs | Professional, direct |
| Email (recruiter/manager) | 3 short paragraphs | Professional |
| Follow-up | 2–4 sentences | Brief, polite, no guilt-tripping |

## Principles

- **Lead with relevance, not the ask.** Why them, why now, then a small ask.
- **One clear ask** per message (a chat, a referral, a status update).
- **Make it easy to say yes** — propose a specific, low-effort next step.
- **Personalize the first line** with something real (their work, a post, a
  shared background). Generic openers get ignored.
- **Respect their time** — shorter is better.

## Templates (adapt, don't paste verbatim)

### connection (≤300 chars)
> Hi [Name] — I'm a [role] focused on [area]. I admire [specific thing about
> their team/work] and would love to connect and follow [Company]'s work.

### recruiter
> Hi [Name], thanks for reaching out about [role]. It looks like a strong match
> for my background in [X] and [Y] — [one quantified proof point]. I'd welcome a
> short call this week. Here's my resume: [link].

### hiring-manager
> Hi [Name], I applied for [role] and wanted to introduce myself directly. I've
> [specific relevant achievement tied to their needs], which maps closely to
> [requirement in the posting]. Happy to share more — would a brief chat work?

### referral
> Hi [Name], hope you're well. I'm applying for [role] at [Company] and saw
> you're connected there. Would you be comfortable referring me or pointing me
> to the right person? I've attached a short blurb to make it easy. No worries
> if not.

### followup
> Hi [Name], following up on my application for [role] from [date]. I'm still
> very interested and happy to provide anything helpful. Thanks for your time.

### thanks
> Hi [Name], thank you for the conversation about [role] today. I especially
> enjoyed discussing [specific topic]. It reinforced my interest in [Company].
> Looking forward to next steps.

## Don't

- No flattery that isn't true.
- No multi-paragraph life stories in a first touch.
- No pressure or repeated follow-ups (one polite nudge, then move on).
