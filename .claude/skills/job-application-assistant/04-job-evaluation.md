# 04 — Job Evaluation (Fit Scoring)

Score fit **before** drafting any materials. Be honest — a weak fit wastes the
user's time. Present the score and recommendation first.

## Inputs

- The job posting (full text; WebFetch the URL or use pasted JD).
- Company research (WebSearch: products, stage, recent news, values).
- The candidate profile (`01`) and behavioral profile (`02`).

## Scoring framework (0–100)

Weight three dimensions:

| Dimension | Weight | What to assess |
|---|---|---|
| **Skills match** | 45% | Required hard skills/tools the candidate genuinely has vs. gaps. Distinguish must-haves from nice-to-haves. |
| **Experience alignment** | 35% | Seniority, domain, scope, and years vs. what the role expects. |
| **Behavioral / cultural fit** | 20% | Work style, values, remote/onsite, company stage vs. `02` preferences. |

Compute a weighted score. Then bucket:

- **80–100 Strong** — apply; tailor aggressively.
- **60–79 Worth it** — apply if interested; address gaps in the cover letter.
- **40–59 Stretch** — only if the user really wants it; be honest about gaps.
- **<40 Skip** — recommend against unless there's a strong non-obvious reason.

## Output

1. Headline score + bucket.
2. **Strengths** — where the candidate clearly matches (cite specific profile
   items and posting requirements).
3. **Gaps / risks** — missing must-haves, seniority mismatch, red lines.
4. **Keywords** to mirror in the resume/cover letter (ATS).
5. **Recommendation** — apply / apply-with-caveats / skip, with one-line reason.

Never inflate a score to be encouraging. Honesty here protects the user.
